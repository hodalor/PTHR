Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PTHR handles information in the web platform (pthr.netlify.app) and the PTHR Mobile Tracking app (Android package com.pthr.mobile).

1. Who is responsible

Your employer or organisation (the PTHR tenant) is the controller of employee, payroll, attendance, and tracking data. PTHR provides the software that stores and processes that data on the organisation’s behalf.

Contact: support@pthr.app

2. Information we process

  • Account data: tenant ID, username or employee ID, name, role, and authentication tokens.
  • Workforce data entered by the organisation: job details, attendance, leave, loans, and payroll figures shown in the app.
  • Precise location: GPS coordinates, accuracy, mock-location flags, and timestamps while you clock in/out or while live tracking is on. This can continue in the background during an active work session.
  • Photos: a camera selfie if your organisation requires clock-in or clock-out photo verification. Photos may include the location at the time of capture.
  • Device data: app platform (Android/iOS), approximate activity state, and whether location services are turned off.
  • Support data: information you send to support, and password-reset codes delivered by email or SMS when requested.

3. Why we use it

  • to sign you in and keep the session secure;
  • to record attendance and verify that clock-in happened at an allowed place;
  • to provide live work tracking to your organisation while tracking is armed;
  • to show your pay summary, deductions, leave, and loan records;
  • to operate, secure, and improve the service.

Location is used for attendance and workforce tracking, not for advertising. Background location is used only while a work tracking session is active.

4. Permissions

  • Location (precise, including background): clock-in verification and live tracking.
  • Camera: optional clock photo required by some organisations.
  • Foreground service: keep location updates running while you work.

5. Sharing

We share data with:

  • your organisation’s authorised PTHR admins, HR, and managers;
  • hosting and infrastructure providers that run the PTHR servers;
  • email or SMS delivery providers when you request a password-reset code;
  • authorities if required by law.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use app data for third-party advertising.

6. Retention

Records are kept for as long as the organisation’s tenant remains active and as required for payroll, attendance, or legal purposes. If your organisation closes the tenant or asks us to delete data, we will delete or anonymise it unless we must keep it by law.

7. Security

Access is protected with tenant-scoped accounts and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so you must also protect your password and device.

8. Children

PTHR is a workplace tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your choices

  • You can refuse location or camera permission, but attendance and tracking features may not work.
  • You can clock out to stop live tracking for that session.
  • You can ask your organisation’s HR/admin to correct or delete your employee records.
  • You can email support@pthr.app for privacy questions.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always at https://pthr.netlify.app/policy.
